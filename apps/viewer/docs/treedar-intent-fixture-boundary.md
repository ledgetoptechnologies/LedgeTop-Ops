# Synthetic TreeDar intent fixture

This fixture explores a typed, capability-fingerprint-bound immutable intent and a close/reopen exact replay. It is not wired to an API, Viewer journal, provider, processing job, or existing migration. Its injected resolver and capability objects are synthetic caller-controlled test doubles, not authenticated authority. SQLite triggers in this disposable test database do not establish production schema integrity, restore lineage, concurrent ownership, crash durability, provenance, or acceptance.

Real TreeDar remains unavailable until a trusted project-scoped immutable asset and receipt resolver, independently validated capabilities, durable grant/ACK and restart recovery are implemented and reviewed. Tests must be repeated against those actual boundaries. Never silently fall back to ODM or enable Gaussian splats. Raw inputs, including survey CSVs, remain retained. No live execution or data is included.
